The PEE Method — How to Structure Every Analysis Paragraph

How to write a PEE paragraph

P
Point (+ name the element) "The use of colour in this artwork is…"
E
Evidence (where in the artwork) "This can be seen in the top left, where…"
E
Explanation (impact on viewer) "This creates a tense/calm/unsettling atmosphere because…"

See it in action — a full example

P
Point The use of colour in Terror (Pemba, 1991) is bold and emotionally charged.
E
Evidence This is visible in the deep red tones dominating the background and the warm ochres on the figures' clothing.
E
Explanation This creates a menacing, violent atmosphere — the viewer senses danger and urgency, which emphasises the human cost of apartheid violence.
